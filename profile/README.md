The **Trustworthy, Robust & Interpretable AI Lab (TRIAL)** is a specialized research node within the **Centre of Excellence for AI and Robotics (CEAIR)** at **Sheffield Hallam University** led by **Dr. Jamie Duell**. 

---

##  Mission Statement

Our mission at **TRIAL** is to pioneer the theoretical foundations, probabilistic models, and practical software required to make artificial intelligence provably reliable, transparent, and resilient. We focus on bridging the gap between complex machine learning frameworks and high-stakes decision-making across cybersecurity, healthcare, and autonomous systems.

---

##  Core Research Pillars

Our research sits at the intersection of machine learning theory and real-world deployment:

*  **Interpretability & Algorithmic Recourse**  
  Developing probabilistic counterfactual explanations and diagnostic feature attributions that explain *why* predictions occur and *how* to safely act.

*  **Model Robustness & Security**  
  Pioneering adversarial defense mechanisms via robust frameworks for agentic AI and autonomous systems.

*  **Uncertainty Quantification**  
  Applying Bayesian neural networks and distribution-shift models to evaluate confidence bounds under model changes, noisy execution, input perturbations and more.

*  **High-Stakes Applications**  
  Translating safety-critical AI into impactful solutions across cybersecurity, network defense, healthcare diagnostics, and biomedical discovery.
